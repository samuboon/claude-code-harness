# Marketplace review (demo report, deliberately imperfect)

The shelf takes a cut of every sale: 「a platform fee of 7.0% of each sale」,
and on top of that 「the payment　processor charges a further 3.5%」.

Money arrives late. 「Payouts are made on the 10th day of the second month
following the sale」, and 「no payout is made below USD 100」.

We can leave: 「thirty days' written notice」, and 「no reason need be given」.

Listing is not instant: 「Review takes 14 business days」.

One more thing worth knowing: 「The Seller Does Not Contract With The Payment
Processor」, and 「Fees are quoted exclusive of tax」.

Watch these two:

- 「a platform fee of 7.5% of each sale」 is what a second pass of the same
  model wrote down.
- 「the seller may withdraw funds at any time without notice」 is in the report
  and in no version of the page.
